GPU Kernel Optimisation

FlashAttention forward kernel

1:1 thread-to-d mapping, shared-memory padding, transposed K/P tile reuse, and warp-level reductions on (B, N, d) = (2000, 512, 32) cut kernel time from 6.74s to 0.576s (11.7×), reaching 99.97% SM efficiency and 3.89 TB/s shared-memory load throughput — 247× faster than the sequential CPU reference.

Blocked Floyd–Warshall APSP

Shared-memory tiling, coalesced global access, and 2×2 register tiling on a 15,000-vertex graph brought solve time from 29.2s to 5.33s (5.5×, 109× over the sequential CPU reference). Sweeping the blocking factor from 8–128 with nvprof found B=64 optimal, peaking at 2.98 TB/s shared-memory bandwidth, with the dominant kernel sustaining 2.28 TB/s at 99.98% SM efficiency. Scaling to two GPUs via P2P cudaMemcpy2DAsync row partitioning held 2.12× scaling against a 2× ideal as the graph grew from N=20,000 to 25,889.

Cross-vendor port — NVIDIA CUDA → AMD ROCm/HIP

Porting both kernels to AMD ROCm/HIP on an MI100, rocprof showed the blocking-factor optimum is vendor-dependent rather than portable: NVIDIA peaked at 2,501 GOPS at B=64, while the MI100 peaked around 105 GOPS at B=16–32 and collapsed by B=64.

Mandelbrot renderer

Dynamic scheduling, SSE2 __m128d intrinsics with mask-controlled early exit, 1024-pixel block partitioning, and manual unrolling brought render time on 8 cores from 367s to 100s (3.7×). A pure-thread version reached 10.6× on 12 threads (88% parallel efficiency), and a hybrid MPI + OpenMP version with interleaved row assignment to balance heterogeneous convergence workloads reached 15.5×.

Distributed odd-even transposition sort

Sorted 536M elements across MPI ranks — binary-search pivots bound exchange volume, MPI_Sendrecv_replace merges in place, and MPI_Allreduce handles early termination. Nsight Systems traced a 3.1× scaling ceiling at 32 processes to communication reaching 45% of runtime.

Wi-Fi CSI Sensing Testbed
Device-free human pose estimation
Linux kernelIntel 5300 802.11 CSIPython

Brought up a legacy Linux Wi-Fi sensing rig by downgrading to kernel 3.14 and building the patched Intel 5300 CSI Tool driver from source, resolving kernel/driver compatibility along the way — enabling per-packet CSI capture over a 2-antenna NIC with a phone hotspot as transmitter.

Found that models trained on public 3-antenna CSI datasets do not transfer to 2-antenna captures, since antenna configuration changes the CSI matrix structure — then built a pipeline to synchronise CSI captures with pose labels for aligned training.
